What it does

Give the road
the final vote.

Choose a Portuguese district and locality, a comfortable distance, and a mood. The roulette selects from more than 130 destinations researched across official tourism pages, independent travel guides, and community recommendations. Every result explains why it matched and links to its inspiration source before handing the exact route to Google Maps.

✓ More than 130 sourced Portuguese destinations
✓ Official, independent, and community inspiration
✓ Coast, nature, history, food, and viewpoint moods
✓ Nearby, day-trip, and weekend distance ranges
✓ Interactive visual route across Portugal
✓ Three-stop mini itinerary for every destination
✓ Curated Google Maps target for every suggested stop
✓ English and Portuguese interface

Surprise without surveillance

The roulette does not request your GPS location. Choose a district and locality manually; saved trips stay in the current browser. Google receives the public place names only if you choose to open directions or a suggested stop.
