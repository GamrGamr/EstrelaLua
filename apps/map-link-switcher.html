What it does

One place.
Your map.

Paste a supported map link, coordinates, an address, or a place name. The switcher extracts the destination locally and creates a fresh link for the navigation service you select.

✓ Google Maps, Waze, Apple Maps, Bing Maps, and OpenStreetMap
✓ Accepts full map links, place names, addresses, and coordinates
✓ Automatically detects destinations while you paste or type
✓ Preserves exact coordinates when the source link contains them
✓ Clear warning when a shortened link hides its destination
✓ No account, API key, installation, or saved history

Converted on this device

The pasted text is processed in the current browser. Nothing is uploaded or stored. A map provider receives the destination only after you choose to open its link.
