## Guide-depth expansion

- **Mini** keeps the three previously verified named landmarks and opens each exact place in Google Maps.
- **Medium** grows What to visit to five suggestions and adds activity and food discovery, giving seven ideas in total.
- **Full guide** grows What to visit to at least seven suggestions, adds more activity, food, and practical discovery, and adapts its total to the destination.
- Coastal destinations receive separate nearby-beach and coastal-lookout cards instead of one ambiguous combined search, producing eight visit suggestions and thirteen ideas overall.
- Every visible category is a native collapsible dropdown and stays compact until the visitor opens it.
- Named landmarks and broader discovery searches use different button labels so the interface never presents a category search as an exact venue.
- Every recommendation repeats the unchanged source link from the guide that inspired the destination. Portuguese guides remain Portuguese; English guides remain English.

## Verification method

- Existing catalogue: full structural audit, starting-locality cross-check, source relevance review, route-link generation checks, and stop-coordinate review.
- Expansions: rate-limited OpenStreetMap/Nominatim lookup followed by manual review of every result. Ambiguous or missing results were replaced with coordinates from destination, municipal, cultural-heritage, tourism, university, or Wikimedia records.
- New destination relevance: all 36 new source articles were checked against their intended destination; all 108 new stop names and coordinates were reviewed and remain within 30 km of the destination they describe.
- Sources: direct live requests for all 131 unique original-language URLs used by the guide cards after integration; all 131 were reachable.
- Maps: structural generation checks for all 1,660 Full-guide links. All 108 new exact-place links and 16 new coastal discovery links also passed a live reachability check; combined with the first audit, all 80 coastal discovery links have been live checked. All 405 exact landmarks have curated place names and coordinate anchors.
- Interface: local browser render, Mini/Medium/Full switching, language-switch checks, generated Google Maps link inspection, and the dependency-free browser test suite.

The live-link result is a point-in-time check. External sites can move or temporarily fail later, so future catalogue expansions should repeat the same audit before publication.
