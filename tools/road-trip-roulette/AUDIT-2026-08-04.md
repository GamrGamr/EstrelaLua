## Verification method

- Existing catalogue: full structural audit, starting-locality cross-check, source relevance review, route-link generation checks, and stop-coordinate review.
- Expansion: rate-limited OpenStreetMap/Nominatim lookup followed by manual review of every result. Ambiguous or missing results were replaced with coordinates from destination, municipal, cultural-heritage, tourism, university, or Wikimedia records.
- Sources: direct live requests for every English and Portuguese source URL after integration.
- Interface: local browser render, language-switch check, generated Google Maps link inspection, console-error check, and the dependency-free browser test suite.

The live-link result is a point-in-time check. External sites can move or temporarily fail later, so future catalogue expansions should repeat the same audit before publication.
