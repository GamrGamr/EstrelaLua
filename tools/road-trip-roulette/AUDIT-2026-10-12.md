## Verification method

- Destination balance and catalogue totals were asserted independently in the browser suite and the integrated bilingual audit.
- New stop coordinates were resolved with rate-limited OpenStreetMap/Nominatim lookups and then checked against the destination radius. Ambiguous results were replaced with reviewed municipal, tourism, cultural-heritage, or public-map coordinates.
- Each of the 36 new destination source pages was opened during research and checked for a successful response and the expected place title.
- Every generated route, exact-place query, bilingual field, supported mood, and guide category was checked programmatically.
- The finished app was rendered locally with a new destination in Portuguese and English. Both interfaces produced the expected copy, sources, Google Maps URLs, and no browser console warnings or errors.

External pages can move after publication, so source availability remains a point-in-time result and should be repeated with future catalogue expansions.
